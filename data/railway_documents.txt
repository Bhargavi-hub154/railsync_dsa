RAILSYNC RAILWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL & INCIDENT DISPATCH LOG
===========================================================

SECTION 1: DISPATCH DIRECTIVES AND PRIORITY CLEARANCE
1.1 All express corridor movements must receive priority clearance over freight services during peak commuter hours.
1.2 When an express train approaches a junction, priority clearance must be logged by the section controller.
1.3 In cases of dual platform conflicts, high-speed rakes hold priority clearance over departmental special trains.
1.4 Priority clearance protocols must be verified by both the station master and the divisional train controller before signal green aspect clearance.
1.5 Freight movements on the trunk corridor may be routed via the loop line to facilitate uninterrupted priority clearance for Rajdhani and Vande Bharat services.

SECTION 2: PLATFORM CONGESTION MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL
2.1 When station dwell time exceeds scheduled limits, platform congestion mitigation measures are triggered immediately.
2.2 Platform congestion at terminal stations must be regulated through automated rake shunting into adjacent holding sidings.
2.3 If platform congestion persists past twenty minutes, trains awaiting platform occupancy must be detained at outer signals.
2.4 Terminal yards must report any developing platform congestion to the central train management system every fifteen minutes.
2.5 Dynamic audio announcements must be activated at concourses to divert passenger footfall during severe platform congestion.

SECTION 3: EMERGENCY TRACK MAINTENANCE AND BLOCK ORDERS
3.1 Scheduled track maintenance requires a minimum four-hour traffic block authorized by the divisional engineer.
3.2 Emergency track maintenance takes precedence over standard timetable commitments to guarantee structural running integrity.
3.3 Ultrasonic rail flaw detection teams conducting track maintenance must deploy banner flags eight hundred meters from the active worksite.
3.4 Heavy mechanised ballast tamping machines engaged in track maintenance must operate under absolute block working rules.
3.5 Following track maintenance completion, an initial speed restriction of thirty kilometers per hour applies until track consolidation is certified.

SECTION 4: SIGNAL FAILURE CONTINGENCY AND FOG PROTOCOL
4.1 In the event of an automatic signal failure, the station master must issue paper authority form T-369-3b to locomotive pilots.
4.2 During dense fog periods, the fog protocol requires visibility test objects to be monitored at the station starter signal.
4.3 Under the active fog protocol, maximum permissible speed for trains equipped with fog safety devices is restricted to seventy-five kilometers per hour.
4.4 Every signal failure incident must be recorded in the signal inspection register and rectified within forty minutes.
4.5 The fog protocol remains in effect until the visibility test object is clearly visible from a distance of one hundred and eighty meters.

SECTION 5: CREW SCHEDULING AND TURNAROUND INSTRUCTIONS
5.1 Locomotive crew working hours shall not exceed nine continuous hours without mandatory relief crew change.
5.2 Fast rake turnaround instructions require incoming and outgoing pilots to conduct joint cab brake feel inspections.
5.3 Adequate crew rest periods are mandatory prior to roster assignment on long-haul passenger expresses.
5.4 Turnaround instructions specify that rake cleaning and watering must be completed within fifty-five minutes of arrival.
5.5 Station dispatchers must log pilot sign-on timestamps and ensure zero deviation from designated crew turnaround instructions.
